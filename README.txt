Sonic Tower

Play Pizza Tower as Sonic the Hedgehog from Sonic Mania. You need both games.
Melty installs and starts it in one click. The first launch builds a separate copy of Pizza Tower's data
from your own games, which takes about a minute. Your games' files are never changed, and no game files are included here.
Sonic Tower keeps its own save files, so your Pizza Tower saves are left alone.
If you have ReShade in your Pizza Tower folder, it doesn't load while you play Sonic Tower because it can crash the game. Your ReShade files and settings are not changed, and it still works when you play Pizza Tower normally.

Controls use your Pizza Tower buttons:
- Left/right: run, with Sonic Mania's momentum
- Jump: jump as a ball that breaks blocks and kills enemies
- Down + jump: spindash. Tap jump to rev, let go of down to release
- Down while moving, or dash: roll
- Hold jump in the air after a jump: drop dash on landing
- Up + jump: super jump
- Down + jump in the air: slam down through floors, with the drop dash's sounds
- Run into a wall at speed: run up it
- Taunt: Sonic strikes a different pose each time, with Pizza Tower's taunt and parry timing
- Up at a door or gate: walk in. Down on a pizza box: go through it

Sound effects come from Sonic Mania where there's a match. Music is Pizza Tower's.

Pick who plays with up and down on the file select, as in Sonic Mania: Sonic and Tails, Sonic, Tails or Knuckles.
- Jump again in the air: Sonic's drop dash (hold jump), Tails's flight (tap jump, he tires after 8 seconds), Knuckles's glide (hold jump, run into a wall to climb, up and down to move, jump to leap off)
- Sonic and Tails: Tails follows along beside Sonic

Whoever you pick stands in for Peppino on the file select, the TV, the results screen, the boss VS art and in the game's words. The tutorial signs and hub tips (in English) teach these characters' moves. Cutscenes drawn as pictures still show Peppino, and transformations play the character's general poses.
